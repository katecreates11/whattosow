## What if

**What if the Night Gardener's one-PR-at-a-time rule needs a second, lighter
path for genuinely trivial fixes?** The rule exists to stop PRs going stale
against `main` — a real problem it solved (see PR #2, late July). But it
also means three dead-link corrections and two two-line data-file additions
are all queued behind the same 17-day review as a whole new interactive
feature. The rule optimises for PR safety; it wasn't designed for a
backlog nine cards deep with a two-week-old known revenue loss sitting in
it. Worth Kate considering whether trivial, low-risk fixes (dead links,
data-file corrections) deserve a different lane than full feature builds.

**What if a fully quiet decision-week, with no plumbing bug left to explain
it, is worth naming honestly rather than filed as "nothing to report"?**
The last two "why has this gone quiet" guesses were both wrong and both
mechanical once checked properly. This week there's no unmerged branch
hiding a decision — the board and PR #3 are genuinely sitting untouched.
That's not evidence of anything beyond "Kate didn't get to it this week,"
and it isn't being turned into a new taste rule on n=1. But it's the kind
of fact this memory exists to hold onto rather than lose, in case it
becomes a pattern.
