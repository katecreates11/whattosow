# Ideas Board

The Forager writes a ranked shortlist here each week. **You (Kate) are the gate:** change a
card's `Status` to `approved`, `parked`, or `binned`. Approved content ideas flow to the
Night Gardener to build; approved features get spec'd first; approved monetisation angles are
usually yours to action. Parked and binned ideas are never re-pitched — this board is the
Forager's memory.

## Status key

- **proposed** — the Forager's pitch, awaiting your call.
- **approved** — yes. Eligible for the Night Gardener (content / spec'd feature) or for you to action.
- **parked** — not now, but not a no. Don't re-pitch.
- **binned** — no. Don't re-pitch.

Feature ideas marked `approved` also get a `needs-spec` note until they've been brainstormed
into a proper build item.

## Card template

```markdown
### [Idea title]
- **Type:** content | feature | monetisation
- **Status:** proposed
- **Score:** payoff/effort (e.g. High payoff / Low effort)
- **Evidence:** <the real query, trend, gap, or data point that prompted this>
- **Pitch:** <what to make and why it wins>
- **Ready-to-queue brief (content only):** target query · angle · internal links · voice notes
- **Added:** YYYY-MM-DD · **Run:** <forager run id>
```
