## The money math (why I'm prioritising the way I am)

Where we actually are, from the last 30 days of Amazon data:

- **21 clicks → 2 sales → £24.48 spent → £0.74 earned.**
- Conversion is **9.5%** — that's excellent. People trust the site and buy. **Trust is not the problem.**
- Two things are the problem: **(1) too few clicks** (traffic + how often we send people to Amazon), and **(2) tiny basket sizes** — 3% of a £3 packet of seeds is 9p; 3% of a £150 raised bed is £4.50. **That's a 50× difference per sale.**

So every idea below is scored against the three real levers, in order of impact:

1. **Higher-value products** — the fastest multiplier. Same number of clicks, 10–50× the commission. *Most neglected on the site right now.*
2. **More traffic** — the ceiling on everything. Pinterest (your weekly job) + SEO content that ranks. *Slow-burn, compounds.*
3. **More clicks per visit** — sending existing readers to Amazon more often, earlier, on the pages that already get traffic. *We've done a lot of this already.*

A key timing fact from the research: **content takes 3–6 months to rank.** It's June. Anything we want to earn from in September–November (autumn sowing, greenhouses, cold frames, Christmas-gift-for-gardeners) **has to be written now.** That single fact shapes the whole calendar.

## TIER 1 — do first (highest £ per hour of my time)

### 1. High-ticket "best X UK" buying guides — *the biggest single opportunity*

Right now the site sells seeds (pennies) and hand tools (a bit better). It sells almost nothing in the £50–£300 bracket, which is exactly where 3% commission becomes real money. The research confirms the demand is there and rising (allotment searches +91% YoY, "home allotment" +40%).

Proposed guides, each in the comparison-table format we already built (`/blog/best-allotment-tools-compared` proved it works):

| Guide | Why | Best season to rank |
|---|---|---|
| **Best raised beds UK** | High AOV, huge spring demand, beginner staple | Rank for Feb–May → write now |
| **Best small greenhouse / cold frame UK** | High AOV, searched in autumn for next year | Rank for Sept–Nov → write now |
| **Best water butt UK** | Mid-high AOV, evergreen, hosepipe-ban spikes | Evergreen |
| **Best compost bin / dalek / hot bin UK** | Mid AOV, ties to our composting guide | Evergreen |
| **Best grow lights / heated propagator UK** | High AOV, searched Dec–Feb for seed starting | Rank for winter → write now |
| **Best polytunnel for an allotment UK** | Very high AOV | Evergreen |

Each guide: honest top pick + 3–4 alternatives, a comparison table, "what to look for" (E-E-A-T), and your first-person take where you own the thing. Internal-linked from the relevant existing guide (greenhouse ↔ seed-starting, water butt ↔ watering, raised beds ↔ soil/composting).

**Effort:** ~1 guide per build session. **Impact: highest.** This is the lever we've barely touched.

> ⚠️ Honesty guardrail (your rule): we only star-rate / "I use this" things you actually own. For kit you don't own, the format becomes "what to look for + the options" rather than a personal review. Still ranks, still earns, stays honest.

### 2. Autumn & winter content — *write now, earn in autumn*

The site has a `spring-vegetables` guide but nothing for the back half of the year, and autumn/winter searches are climbing ("winter vegetables to plant" +21%). Because of the 3–6 month lag, **now is exactly the time.**

- **Guide: "What to sow in autumn (UK)"** — quick crops (radish, oriental leaves, lambs' lettuce, spinach) + overwintering (broad beans, hardy peas, autumn onions/garlic). Each crop links to its page + seeds (Awin) + relevant kit (cloches, fleece, garlic — all affiliate).
- **Guide: "Overwintering vegetables / extending the season"** — pairs perfectly with the cold-frame & greenhouse buying guides above (cross-sell the high-ticket kit).
- **"Autumn allotment jobs" + "winter allotment jobs"** diary-style posts (your voice, your photos) → pin them → carry kit links (leaf-mould bags, garlic, fleece, cloches).

**Effort:** medium. **Impact:** seasonal traffic we currently get *none* of, feeding the Tier-1 high-ticket guides.

## TIER 2 — the traffic engine (grows the ceiling)

### 3. Build out the companion-planting cluster

Companion planting is our #1 page by a mile (558/90d). The research shows topic clusters build authority: satellite pages that each rank for a long-tail term *and* funnel link-equity to the hub. We've made the hub excellent — now give it children:

- "Companion plants for tomatoes" (and carrots, beans, brassicas, courgettes — the high-traffic crops)
- "What NOT to plant together" (high search, high curiosity, very pinnable)
- "Companion planting for small gardens / raised beds" (ties to the raised-bed buying guide)
- "Flowers for the veg patch" (marigolds, nasturtiums, borage, calendula — all seed-affiliate, you have gorgeous photos)

Each links up to the hub and carries seed + flower affiliate links. This is how the single best page becomes ten good pages.

**Effort:** medium (we have the photos and the hub to model on). **Impact:** grows our strongest asset.

### 4. Make Pinterest easier to do at scale

Pinterest is the #1 traffic lever and it's *your* weekly job. The research is blunt about what works now:

- **8–12 fresh pins/day** is the sweet spot (you're doing "a few weekly" — there's headroom).
- **Fresh image = fresh pin**, even on the same URL. New creative revives old posts.
- **Idea Pins get ~9× the saves** of standard pins (multi-page tips/tutorials).
- **Saves are the #1 signal** — design for "I want to keep this."

What I can build to make that realistic for you:
- More pin **templates/variants** in the pin engine so every post can throw off 3–4 genuinely different fresh images (we have 2 styles now) — turns "a few weekly" into "a fresh one a day" with no extra design work from you.
- **Tip/quote pins** auto-generated from guide content (e.g. "5 things to sow in October") — these are the Idea-Pin-style savers.
- A simple **weekly pinning checklist** on `/pins` ("pin these 7 this week") so it's a 10-minute Sunday job, not a decision.

**Effort:** medium. **Impact:** multiplies your existing weekly effort — this is leverage on the one thing only you can do.

## What I'd do if you only picked three

1. **Two high-ticket buying guides now** — one spring-timed (raised beds), one autumn-timed (small greenhouse/cold frame). This is the 50× commission multiplier and nobody on the site is doing it yet.
2. **The autumn-sowing guide now** — so we actually capture the Sept–Nov season instead of missing it again.

## What I deliberately left off

- **More features** (games, planners, trackers). Fun, but they don't move affiliate income and we've established traffic — not features — is the ceiling.
- **A live £-raised counter.** You removed it on purpose; not re-adding.
- **Chasing Amazon's 3% with a pivot to other programmes.** Awin/Amazon mix is fine; the win is higher-value items + more clicks, not a new network.
- **Blog index magazine redesign.** Low leverage for money (won't drive visits or clicks).
