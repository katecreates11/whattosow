# The backlink kit — making distribution a two-minute job (July 2026)

Why this exists: Google is only ~15% of search traffic and 130 pages sit in
"Discovered – not indexed". Authority is the bottleneck, and a handful of genuine
links moves it. Everything here is designed so one outreach = one ten-minute paste.

**Rules:** never pay, never link-swap schemes, always lead with the thing that helps
*their* readers, one asset per contact (the most relevant one), and keep a little
log (who / date / asset / reply). Two contacts a week is plenty — 8–10 real links by
autumn changes the crawl story.

## The linkable assets (lead with tools, not articles)

1. **The frost map** (`/frost-map`) — free postcode frost dates for the whole UK.
   The single most linkable thing on the site; nothing else free does this.
2. **The printable companion chart** (`/guides/companion-planting-chart`) — made to
   be pinned to noticeboards. Societies and community gardens love a printable.
3. **Blight Watch** (`/blight-watch`) — live UK blight risk. Seasonal gold July–Sept;
   pitch it in blight weather when it's genuinely urgent news.
4. **The harvest planner** (`/harvest-planner`) — for the "useful links" pages.

## The targets, easiest first

1. **Your own allotment society** (and the neighbouring ones) — most have a Links or
   Resources page, and you're a member; this is a warm ask. → Email A.
2. **Allotment & gardening society newsletters** (parish, county horticultural
   societies, garden clubs) — editors are perpetually short of content; give them a
   ready-made blurb. → Blurb B.
3. **National Allotment Society regional reps / newsletter** — same blurb, bigger
   reach; mention you're a plot-holder member if you are.
4. **UK gardening forums** (e.g. allotment-garden.org) and **r/GardeningUK** — make
   a real profile, answer real questions, and link the frost map only when someone
   literally asks "when is my last frost?" Helpfulness first; links follow.
5. **Facebook allotment groups** — share the chart or Blight Watch in season
   (traffic + discovery rather than SEO, but discovery is how links start).
6. **Local press / community newsletters** — "local plot-holder builds free frost
   tool for UK gardeners" is a genuine local-interest story. → Pitch C.
7. **Community gardens & school gardening clubs** — the printable chart, offered
   outright. A .gov.uk or .sch.uk link is a quiet prize.

## Email A — society links page (personalise the [brackets])

> Subject: A free frost-date tool for the [SOCIETY NAME] links page
>
> Hello [NAME],
>
> I'm a plot-holder [at SITE / member] and I run a small free website called What
> To Sow — it works out last and first frost dates for any UK postcode, and what
> to sow each week based on them. No ads, no sign-up, just a tool I built because
> I wanted it to exist: https://whattosow.co.uk/frost-map
>
> If you think members would find it useful, I'd be chuffed if it went on the
> links page. And if you'd like a printable companion-planting chart for the
> noticeboard, there's one here: https://whattosow.co.uk/guides/companion-planting-chart
>
> Either way — happy growing,
> Kate

## Blurb B — ready-made newsletter snippet (they paste, job done)

> **Free tool: frost dates for your exact postcode.** What To Sow
> (whattosow.co.uk) is a free UK site built by an allotment holder that works out
> your local last-frost date, what to sow each week, and even live potato-blight
> risk for your area. No ads or sign-ups — worth a bookmark for the shed.

## Pitch C — local press / community magazine (3 sentences)

> Hello — I'm a [TOWN] allotment holder and I've built a free website that tells
> UK gardeners their local frost dates and what to sow each week, personalised to
> their postcode (whattosow.co.uk). It started as a tool for my own plot and now
> has live blight warnings, a printable companion-planting chart and a UK frost
> map. If that might interest your readers this growing season, I'd be happy to
> share more or write a short piece.
