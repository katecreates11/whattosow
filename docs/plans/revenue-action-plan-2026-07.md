# Revenue action plan - July 2026

Goal: find the fastest trust-safe path to the shed fund from existing traffic,
existing intent, and existing commercial surfaces.

This is not a plan for more affiliate links everywhere. What To Sow earns by
being useful first: the postcode Answer, watering note, crop verdicts, FAQs,
problem clinics and charm moments stay commercial-free. The commercial work
happens where the reader is already trying to buy, solve a practical problem, or
choose seeds.

Baseline: the 7 July affiliate measurement report is directional, not decisive.
It shows 3.24k visitors this year, 72 commercial-ish events, and early evidence
that gear/kit clicks are stronger than seed clicks. Suttons and Thompson &
Morgan are visible in the data; Amazon is not the only useful merchant.

The rule: no new affiliate surface unless it replaces, tests against, or removes
an existing one.

## The commercial read

The fastest path is not a shop. It is:

1. Keep the core promise clean so readers trust the page.
2. Use high-intent pages where the reader already has a problem or buying job.
3. Replace weak generic buttons with specific, editorial buying advice.
4. Measure for 30 days before expanding anything.

The highest-value pattern in the current data is specific need plus relevant
product: water storage on watering pages, mesh where pests are the real problem,
seed suppliers on crop and sowing pages, and kit where the page is explicitly a
buying guide.

## Pages where affiliate links should not be added

- `/` homepage trust anchors: postcode Answer, Tonight's Watering Note, the
  Week's List intro, masthead, dateline, email capture and FAQ.
- `/frost-map`: high trust utility; keep it as proof and route people to crop
  or sow pages for commercial decisions.
- `/calendar`: navigational/trust page; use it to move readers to `/sow` and
  crop pages, not to sell.
- `/my-garden` and `/my-plot`: personal pages; no commercial expansion, and
  `/my-garden` still needs noindex/sitemap hygiene before any growth work.
- Crop verdict bands and "Can I sow this now?" answer blocks on all crop pages.
- Problem clinics unless the product genuinely is the standard fix, and the
  free fix leads first.
- FAQs, empty states, 404/charm moments, email capture, about/privacy pages.
- `/still-time`: demotion candidate; do not invest in new monetisation there.
- Thin or private admin/generated/pin routes.

   - Measure clicks by crop, merchant and position for 30 days.
   - Rewrite weak labels before adding links elsewhere.

2. **Crop-page rail and inline seed links**
   - The cut list warns of duplicate inline seed links on desktop.
   - Keep mobile usefulness, but avoid rail plus inline plus variety cards all
     shouting in the same screenful.

3. **Worth-buying / skip-this blocks**
   - Already the strongest trust-safe pattern.
   - Improve the five existing crops first: tomatoes, carrots, courgettes,
     maincrop potatoes, runner beans.
   - Do not roll out to every crop until the 30-day report shows which formats
     earn clicks without clutter.

4. **Buying guides and kit guides**
   - Replace generic button copy with specific buy-lines.
   - Do not change affiliate URLs or tracking while restyling.
   - Prune products that had traffic and zero clicks after one fair reporting
     period.

5. **Seasonal kit edit**
   - Useful as an editorial shop moment, but it appears on multiple surfaces.
   - Keep it to at most two surfaces and use the report to decide which two.

6. **Blight Watch defence kit**
   - Leave it problem-led and restrained.
   - Improve clarity only; do not add more fear-adjacent products.

## First five money-focused commits

Each commit should be small enough to review and should either improve an
existing commercial moment or remove a weak one.

### 1. Inventory and prune duplicate commercial surfaces

Deliverable: a docs-backed inventory of affiliate links by page, merchant,
product, type and position, compared against the Umami report.

Change: remove or hide obvious duplicate seed surfaces where the same screenful
already has a better link, especially desktop crop-page duplication.

Why first: pruning before adding protects trust and makes later measurement
cleaner.

### 2. Restyle generic product buttons into editorial buy-lines

Deliverable: no grey marketplace furniture where the page is editorial.

Change: update existing `AffiliateLink` usages in guides/rails to read like
specific advice: product name plus why it earns its place. Preserve URLs,
`rel=sponsored noopener noreferrer`, and every `data-umami-*` attribute.

Why second: this can lift clicks without increasing link count.

### 3. Tighten /sow SEEDS measurement and copy

Deliverable: the `/sow` listings page remains one commercial column, but the
seed links are clearer and measurable by crop/position.

Change: improve weak link text and verify all seed links use the unified
`affiliate-click` fields. Do not add more seed blocks.

Why third: `/sow` is the page most aligned to seed buying, but the redesign needs
a clean 30-day read.

### 4. Improve the top two problem-led guide moments

Deliverable: one trust-led commercial moment each on `/guides/watering` and
`/blight-watch`, using the products already justified by the advice.

Change: rewrite copy around existing links so the free fix leads and the product
is the practical next step. Prune any generic or duplicated button nearby.

Why fourth: these pages show the clearest "I need a thing" intent.

### 5. Refine existing crop worth-buying / skip-this blocks

Deliverable: tomatoes and carrots become the pattern others can copy, without
expanding to all crops yet.

Change: sharpen the worth/skip copy, keep skip entries unlinked, and remove any
nearby duplicate commercial moment that competes with the block.

Why fifth: crop pages are the long-term revenue engine, but only if the advice
feels earned.

## What to measure after 30 days

Use the affiliate measurement runbook. Pull the same 30-day window from Umami
and merchant dashboards.

Minimum report:

| Metric | Decision it supports |
|---|---|
| Affiliate clicks by page/path | Which pages deserve commercial polish and which do not. |
| Sessions by page/path | Clicks per 1,000 sessions, not just raw clicks. |
| Affiliate clicks by merchant | Whether Amazon, Suttons or Thompson & Morgan are earning trust. |
| Revenue by merchant | Whether clicks are turning into shed-fund money. |
| Affiliate clicks by product | Which practical problems convert. |
| Affiliate clicks by type | Whether seeds or gear are doing the work. |
| Affiliate clicks by position | Rail vs inline vs listings vs variety-card. |
| Top 10 links | What to protect and improve. |
| Zero-click links with page sessions | What to prune or rewrite. |
| Pages with affiliate links but no clicks | Where commerce is clutter. |

The 30-day decision rules:

- Keep a link if it has clicks, fits the page intent, and does not compete with
  a stronger nearby commercial moment.
- Rewrite a link if the product is right but the copy is generic.
- Prune a link if the page had meaningful sessions and zero clicks for a full
  reporting period.
- Do not declare a merchant winner without revenue data.
- Do not expand worth-buying/skip blocks sitewide from one crop's result.
- Do not add a second commercial moment to a screenful unless one existing
  moment is removed.

## The safest next commercial experiment

The safest experiment is not a new block. It is a replacement test:

Replace one weak generic product button on a high-intent guide with one specific
editorial buy-line, keeping the same destination and tracking. Measure clicks
per 1,000 sessions for 30 days against the previous baseline.

Best candidate: `/guides/watering`, because watering gear has clear intent and
the report already shows gear clicks outperforming seed clicks.

Success would mean better copy and placement can earn more without increasing
commercial density. Failure means the link can be pruned without harm.

## What not to do

- Do not add affiliate links to the postcode Answer, Watering Note, crop
  verdicts, FAQs or charm moments.
- Do not build a product wall.
- Do not add a new dashboard, tool or `/my-garden` work for revenue.
- Do not use fake urgency.
- Do not use generic Amazon button copy.
- Do not expand all crop buying blocks until the current ones have a 30-day
  read.
- Do not keep zero-click links because they might earn someday.
- Do not call a recommendation "the one we use" unless Kate has confirmed it.

The shed fund grows fastest when the site feels least like it is chasing it.
