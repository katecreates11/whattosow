## FIX NOW

Ranked by impact. Each names the exact file/line and the change.

1. **Homepage has no page-specific title or meta description.** `src/app/page.tsx` has no `metadata`/`generateMetadata` export at all — it silently inherits the root `layout.tsx:28-30` title ("What To Sow — UK Planting Calendar by Postcode") and description. That's the single most important page on the site running on a generic fallback instead of a tuned title/description. Add a dedicated `metadata` export to `page.tsx`.

2. **Homepage renders two identical `<h1>` elements.** `src/app/page.tsx:265` and `:295` both render "Know exactly what to plant, right now" — one in a `sm:hidden` mobile block, one in a `hidden sm:flex` desktop overlay. Both exist in the raw server HTML regardless of viewport (only CSS toggles visibility), so crawlers see two H1s on the homepage. Same technique appears on `harvest-planner/page.tsx:44,54` and `print/page.tsx:82,101`, gated by `print:hidden`/`print:block` — lower-risk since it's print-only, but worth the same fix if touching this pattern.

3. **`/lucky-dip` self-canonicalizes to `/my-garden`, a different live page.** `src/app/lucky-dip/page.tsx:21` sets `alternates: { canonical: "/my-garden" }` and `openGraph.url` to the `/my-garden` URL. Unless this is a deliberate consolidation (not documented anywhere), Google will treat `/lucky-dip` as a duplicate of `/my-garden` and may drop it from the index entirely. Combined with the near-duplicate titles below, this reads like leftover debris from the my-plot/my-garden/lucky-dip triplication CUT_LIST already flags for merging (⛔ gated on Kate) — until that merge happens, fix the canonical to point at itself or the fix will actively suppress one of the two pages.

4. **`/my-garden` and `/lucky-dip` have near-duplicate titles.** `my-garden/page.tsx:6`: "My Garden — Your Allotment Dashboard | What To Sow"; `lucky-dip/page.tsx:6`: "My Allotment — Your Garden Dashboard | What To Sow" — same four words reshuffled, descriptions both open "Your personalised allotment dashboard…". Same underlying problem as #3.

5. **`/my-garden` and `/lucky-dip` have no H1 at all.** Both routes render only `<Header />` + `<ReactGarden />` (`my-garden/page.tsx:26-33`, `lucky-dip/page.tsx:26-35`); `ReactGarden.tsx` starts its heading hierarchy at h2 ("📌 Allotment Noticeboard", line 270). Two indexed, metadata-rich routes with zero H1.

6. **Titles and descriptions run long across almost the entire guides fleet.** Roughly 27 of ~31 guide pages exceed the ~60-character title budget (worst offenders: `guides/dealing-with-the-glut/page.tsx:12` at 91 chars, `guides/overwintering-broad-beans-and-peas/page.tsx:12` at 85, `guides/protecting-vegetables-from-frost/page.tsx:43` at 83, `guides/companion-planting-chart/page.tsx:8` at 82). About 20 guide pages exceed 160-character descriptions (worst: `guides/protecting-vegetables-from-frost/page.tsx:43` at 209, `guides/growing-root-vegetables/page.tsx:12` at 213, `guides/watering-while-away/page.tsx:11` at 214, `bed-planner/page.tsx:7` at 215). Google truncates both in the SERP snippet with "…", which weakens the click-through pitch on nearly every guide. Worth a batch tightening pass rather than one-by-one — same defect, same fix, repeated ~40 times.

7. **`/sow-in/[city]` has heading stutter, on all ~51 pages.** H1 at `sow-in/[city]/page.tsx:237-239` reads "What to sow in {city.name}"; H2 further down at `:318-319` reads "What to sow in {city.name} right now" — the exact "two headings, one idea" pattern ANTI_PATTERNS.md names. Reword the H2 to something that earns a distinct job (e.g. what it actually introduces: the crop list for the current month).

8. **`/blight-watch` has no structured data at all.** Every sibling route carries at minimum `BreadcrumbList`; `blight-watch/page.tsx` has none. It also has content that maps cleanly to schema already used elsewhere on the site — the "Stay ahead of it" / "If it strikes" tip lists (lines 97-124) and the resistant-variety comparison table (lines 136-160+) — and its title is 68 chars (`blight-watch/page.tsx:11`), over budget.

9. **Broken breadcrumb JSON-LD on every crop and variety page.** `crops/[slug]/page.tsx:495` and `crops/[slug]/[variety]/page.tsx:66` both emit a `BreadcrumbList` item pointing to `https://whattosow.co.uk/#explore-crops`. No `id="explore-crops"` exists anywhere in the codebase (confirmed by grep) — this is a dead anchor instead of the real `/crops` index. Low SEO cost (breadcrumbs are informational, not a ranking signal) but it's shipping broken markup on ~230 pages (48 crops + up to 181 varieties) for a one-line fix.

10. **Variety pages (`crops/[slug]/[variety]/page.tsx`) carry only `BreadcrumbList`.** No `Article`, `HowTo`, or `Product` schema, despite each page having genuinely unique variety-specific prose (the "personality" paragraph, `page.tsx:130`, avg. 38 words) and a recipe. The crop-page template one level up (`crops/[slug]/page.tsx`) already combines `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` + `HowTo` correctly — worth reusing that pattern down at the variety level.

11. **10 of ~31 guide pages are missing `Article` schema.** `allotment-essentials`, `beginners`, `composting`, `crop-rotation`, `growing-fruit`, `pests`, `seed-starting`, `seed-starting-kit`, `soil`, `watering` (all `src/app/guides/*/page.tsx`) currently carry only `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`. Every other guide in the fleet (e.g. `growing-brassicas`, `succession-sowing`) adds `Article` with `author`/`publisher` — these 10 are the odd ones out, missing `datePublished`/author attribution for no apparent reason.

12. **`guides/tomato-blight/page.tsx` has an unmarked 4-step how-to.** The prevention steps at lines 41-44 ("Improve airflow" / "Keep water off the foliage" / "Check, then remove and bin" / "Harvest ahead of it", rendered at line 152) are a clean `HowTo`/`HowToStep` candidate but the page only carries `Article` + `Organization`. It also has no `FAQPage`, despite crop pages using that exact FAQ pattern already.

13. **No sitewide `Organization` schema.** `Organization` only appears nested as `author`/`publisher` inside individual pages' JSON-LD — there's no top-level Organization declaration (logo, `sameAs`, etc.) anywhere, including `layout.tsx`. This is what feeds a Google Knowledge Panel / brand entity; worth adding once (in the root layout) rather than per-page.

14. **`keywords` meta arrays are still present in ~53 files.** CUT_LIST.md already says to stop — Google has ignored this tag since 2009. Full list from the audit: `layout.tsx`, `crops/[slug]/page.tsx`, `sow-in/[city]/page.tsx`, `sow/[month]/page.tsx`, `blog/[slug]/page.tsx`, `guides/companion-planting/[topic]/page.tsx`, `calendar`, `blight-watch`, `about`, `sow-in`, `blog`, `lucky-dip`, `bed-planner`, `products`, `my-garden`, `longest-day`, `grow-your-christmas-dinner`, `still-time`, `harvest-planner`, `kit`, `frost-map`, `guides` (index) plus ~30 individual `guides/*/page.tsx` files. Harmless to leave, cheap to strip in a batch pass — and worth banning from new pages going forward (Codex/Night Gardener keep re-adding these per CUT_LIST §8).

## OPPORTUNITIES

Evidence-based, current UK gardening season (mid-July → early autumn).

1. **The site already has July's core content — the job now is indexing + internal linking, not new pages.** Web search confirms mid-July UK high-intent queries cluster around tomato blight (warm/humid weather triggers it now), courgette/tomato glut and preserving, and evening watering during dry spells. `/guides/tomato-blight`, `/guides/dealing-with-the-glut`, and `/guides/watering-while-away` already cover exactly this — but two of the three aren't showing as indexed yet (see INDEXING above) and none of them are cross-linked from the crop pages most likely to send that traffic (e.g. `/crops/tomatoes` and `/crops/courgettes` should be linking into the blight and glut guides contextually, not just via the generic guides index).

2. **Spring cabbage is a named August highlight with no dedicated page.** Multiple sources call out spring cabbage as one of August's most valuable sowings (sow now in modules, transplant Sept/Oct, harvest March-May) — a genuinely different sowing story from summer cabbage. `src/data/crops.ts` has one generic `cabbage` slug with no spring-cabbage-specific treatment, and `/guides/growing-brassicas` doesn't call it out as its own subsection either. Near-miss worth a paragraph/subhead addition to the cabbage crop page or the brassicas guide ahead of the August sowing window, rather than a whole new page.

3. **Autumn garlic is a live commercial + editorial window Kate has already flagged as a priority** (per `docs/dreams/wisdom.md` — she approved a garlic/onion content pitch citing "a good focus on getting those affiliate clicks as well as useful content" for the autumn pre-order window). Only `/guides/growing-onions-garlic-leeks` covers garlic today, as one of three crops in a general guide — no dedicated garlic hub page has shipped yet. Garlic pre-orders typically open through late summer for September/October planting, so there's a real lead-time window now to get a page built and indexed before the buying window opens. This is a content-build decision for Kate/the Forager pipeline, not something this report changes — flagging it here because it's a live SEO opportunity, not a new idea.

4. **Winter salad leaves (corn salad, land cress, chicory) are already well covered** — `/guides/growing-winter-salad-leaves` exists and matches this season's search demand. No gap found here; noting it so it isn't re-pitched.
