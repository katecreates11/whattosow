## OPPORTUNITIES

Evidence-based, current UK gardening season (mid-to-late August, moving into September). Cross-checked against `docs/ideas-board.md` first so nothing here duplicates a card already on the board.

1. **Drought/hosepipe-ban content — zero coverage on the site right now, and the evidence has sharpened considerably since it was last scoped.** Card 12 ("Allotments and the hosepipe ban") was approved 22 July on evidence of ~11m people under restriction across three water companies; card 16 (postcode-aware version) approved 4 August. Neither is built yet. As of this week: **ten water companies** now have Temporary Use Bans in force (Wessex Water's started 18 August), **71.3% of England is in drought**, and **27m+ people** are under restriction — England's driest July on record. This isn't a new pitch (it's already on the board, correctly scoped), but it's worth flagging here because the gap is real today and the urgency has materially increased since it was queued — the single site search for hosepipe content today (`site:whattosow.co.uk hosepipe`) returns nothing.
2. **Spring cabbage overwintering — near-miss on an existing page.** Early-to-mid August is the sowing window for spring cabbage (sow now → overwinter as small plants → harvest March-May, weeks before any spring-sown crop comes in — a real, distinct technique from summer/autumn cabbage). The `cabbage` crop entry (`src/data/crops.ts`) doesn't name this window or technique in its tip, and its variety list (Hispi, January King, Red Drumhead) doesn't include the actual overwintering cultivars gardeners search for by name — Durham Early, April, Flower of Spring, Wintergreen. A tip addition plus 2-3 named varieties would close a real gap on a page that already exists and already ranks for "cabbage."
3. **Allium leaf miner / leek moth — near-miss, seasonal window opens now.** The danger period is March-June and **September-November** — opening in the next few weeks. `growing-onions-garlic-leeks` already mentions "allium leaf miner & onion fly" with general mesh advice, but doesn't name the September-November window specifically or the mesh spec that matters (0.8mm fine mesh, not the standard 1.3mm "fine" mesh sold as insect-proof, which the RHS says isn't reliable against this pest). The `pests` guide doesn't mention allium leaf miner or leek moth at all, despite being the page most likely to catch a generic "allium leaf miner UK" search. A precise addition to one or both pages, timed to land before the autumn window opens, would be a small, well-evidenced fix.
