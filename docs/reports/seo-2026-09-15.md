This week's pass went deeper than previous weeks on three fronts that hadn't been checked before:
structured data (JSON-LD) coverage, duplicate/thin content on the 181 variety pages, and rough
indexing checks on individual pages (not just the site as a whole). Two genuinely new, high-value
findings came out of that: **`/crops/tomatoes` — one of the site's biggest head-term pages —
doesn't appear to be indexed**, and **all 181 variety pages duplicate their parent crop page's
text word-for-word**, which is a stronger version of the "thin content" risk CUT_LIST already had
these pages on watch for.

## FIX NOW

### 1. The guide/topic/city title-and-description batch — still the single biggest lever, now with the full scope mapped
Unchanged in substance from the last three reports, but this week's pass found two more page
families with the exact same root cause, so the real batch is larger than previously stated:

- **24 of 33 individual `/guides/[slug]` pages** have titles over ~60 characters (up to 79 chars),
  and **26 of 33** have descriptions over ~160 characters (up to 233 chars — nearly 50% over
  budget). Worst offenders unchanged from last week: `growing-root-vegetables` (81-char title),
  `growing-squash-pumpkins-courgettes` (80), `growing-brassicas` (80), `tomato-blight` (78),
  `autumn-planting-garlic-onions` (233-char description).
- **New this week — all 17 `/guides/companion-planting/[topic]` pages** have the identical
  problem (65–76 char titles, 181–215 char descriptions), and it's a single data-file fix:
  `src/data/companion-topics.ts` — one `metaTitle`/`description` field per topic, ~17 lines total.
- **New this week — the `/sow-in/[city]` description template guarantees overlength for all 51
  cities.** `src/app/sow-in/[city]/page.tsx:47` — the template skeleton alone is 151 characters
  *before* the city name, county name or frost date are substituted in, so every one of the 51
  pages goes over 160 characters. One template edit fixes all 51 at once.
- **`/sow` itself is 171 characters** (`src/app/sow/page.tsx:16`) — one of the two or three most
  important pages on the site (sitemap priority 0.9) and currently over budget.

**Fix (same shape for all of the above):** drop either the "(UK Guide)" qualifier or the
"| What To Sow" suffix from titles — not both — and trim descriptions to end on a full sentence
around 150 characters. This is genuinely one afternoon across ~102 pages (33 guides + 17 topics +
51 cities + `/sow`), not 102 separate decisions — three data/template edits and one page edit.

### 2. `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` is still a true orphan, and the seasonal clock is now the loudest it's been
Re-confirmed by grepping every file in `src/app` and `src/components`: the string
`grow-your-christmas-dinner` appears only inside the page's own file and the sitemap entry — zero
inbound links from anywhere else on the site. This week's indexing check (below) confirms it still
doesn't surface in search. Today is 15 September — the Nov/Dec "growing your own Christmas dinner"
search window is roughly 8–10 weeks out, which is close to the lead time Google typically needs to
crawl, index and start ranking a newly-linked page. **This is the last report where "not urgent
yet" is a defensible read of this finding.** Fix (unchanged): link from
`/guides/growing-brassicas` (sprouts), `/guides/preparing-your-plot-for-winter`, and
`/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions`, plus a homepage/footer seasonal nudge from now through
December.

### 3. New finding — all 181 variety pages duplicate their parent crop page's text word-for-word
Every variety page's entire unique content budget (the `personality` paragraph and 1–2 recipe
descriptions, ~100–150 words total, from `src/data/varieties.ts`) is *also* rendered verbatim on
the parent `/crops/[slug]` page:
- `src/app/crops/[slug]/page.tsx:854` (`<p>{v.personality}</p>`) duplicates
  `src/app/crops/[slug]/[variety]/page.tsx:125` exactly.
- `src/app/crops/[slug]/page.tsx:865` (`{r.description}`) duplicates
  `src/app/crops/[slug]/[variety]/page.tsx:147` exactly.

So the only text that makes a variety page unique is also fully present, word-for-word, on a
different indexed URL that outranks it on authority. This sharpens CUT_LIST §4's existing "on
watch — thin, prune if Google won't index" note into something more specific: it isn't just thin,
it's near-duplicate against the page most likely to win the same query. **Fix:** either stop
rendering full variety personality/recipe text on the crop page (teaser + link out instead), or
treat this as evidence toward CUT_LIST's pruning decision on the 181-page cluster — Kate's call,
flagging with the ⛔ marker per CUT_LIST convention since it's a content-removal decision either way.

### 4. Structured data gaps on pages that matter
Coverage is generally good (most guides carry `Article`+`FAQPage`+`BreadcrumbList`; crop pages
carry `HowTo`/`FAQPage`; the homepage has `WebApplication`+`FAQPage`), but several high-priority
pages have none of that:
- **`/sow`** (sitemap priority 0.9, the personalised weekly list) — **no JSON-LD at all**.
- **`/sow/[month]`** (12 evergreen pages targeting "what to sow in [month] UK") — only
  `BreadcrumbList`; genuinely content-rich pages that would benefit from `Article` or `FAQPage`.
- **`/crops`** index (~48 crops) — **no JSON-LD at all**; a natural `ItemList`.
- **`/frost-map`, `/grow`, `/harvest`, `/harvest-planner`, `/us`** — no JSON-LD.
- **7 individual guides** (`beginners`, `crop-rotation`, `growing-fruit`, `pests`,
  `seed-starting`, `soil`, `watering`) have `FAQPage`+`Breadcrumb` but, unlike the other 26 guides,
  no `Article`/publisher schema — an inconsistency worth closing for template consistency.
- **Variety pages** (181 of them) carry only `BreadcrumbList` — a lightweight `Product` (with the
  seed-supplier `Offer`) would be a natural, low-effort add given these are buy-intent pages.

### 5. `/privacy` is still indexable but still missing from the sitemap
Unchanged for the third week running. `src/app/privacy/page.tsx` has a real title/description, no
noindex, and is linked from the footer — it just isn't in `src/app/sitemap.ts`. One-line fix:
```ts
{ url: `${baseUrl}/privacy`, lastModified: new Date("2026-04-02"), changeFrequency: "yearly", priority: 0.3 },
```

### 6. `/sow-in/[city]` titles change every month and year for no reason
New finding: `src/app/sow-in/[city]/page.tsx:46` bakes the current month and year into the
`<title>` itself (`What to Sow in ${city.name} — ${monthName} ${year} Sowing Guide`), and the page
revalidates hourly. That means Google sees a different title for the same URL every calendar
month, for all 51 cities, even though the page's actual crop list already updates without needing
a title change. One-line fix: drop month/year from the `<title>`, keep it in the on-page copy.

### 7. Housekeeping — bundle into the pass above
- **57 files** now carry a legacy `keywords` meta array (was 55 last week) — CUT_LIST already
  calls this decided policy (Google has ignored the tag since 2009). Full list gathered this week
  spans `layout.tsx`, all 33 guides, all 17 companion-planting topics (`src/data/companion-topics.ts`),
  27 editorial posts (`src/data/editorial-posts.ts`), and most core route pages. Delete while
  editing these files for #1 anyway.
- **Two pages render two `<h1>` elements each**: homepage (`src/app/page.tsx:269` and `:299`,
  identical text, CSS-toggled mobile/desktop — unchanged from last week) and, new this week,
  **`/harvest-planner`** (`src/app/harvest-planner/page.tsx:44` and `:54`, screen vs. print
  versions, different text). Low priority, same fix both times: one `<h1>` with responsive styling.
- **`/kit` still has the wrong canonical flagged weeks ago** (`alternates.canonical: "/guides"` at
  `src/app/kit/page.tsx:30`, `openGraph.url` also pointing at `/guides`) — but this week's check
  found it's functionally moot: `next.config.ts:6` permanently redirects `/kit` → `/guides`, so the
  page and its bad metadata are never actually served. Recommend deleting the dead page file, or
  fixing the canonical now so it's correct the moment anyone lifts the redirect.
- **Naming inconsistency, not a same-page stutter:** the `/crops` destination is called "Explore
  crops" in the footer, "Browse the crops" on the 404 page, and "The crops" as its own page title —
  three names for one page across three surfaces. Not the CUT_LIST-documented same-page stutter
  (that pattern wasn't found live this week — may already be partly cleaned up), but worth
  converging on one name.

## INDEXING (approximate — no Search Console access)

This week's check went page-by-page rather than site-wide, and surfaced a clearer pattern:
**crop and guide page templates index well; location (`/sow-in`) and monthly (`/sow/[month]`)
templates index patchily.** Treat every "no evidence found" below as a prompt to check the real
Search Console Coverage report, not proof of deindexing.

- **`/sow/september` — no evidence found, despite `/sow/august` and `/sow/february` both indexing
  fine.** This is the current month's page. Highest-priority indexing gap this week — request
  indexing now while it's live.
- **`/crops/tomatoes` — no evidence found**, which is genuinely surprising: smaller-demand crops
  (peas, brussels sprouts, rocket, broccoli) all index fine, but the site's biggest head-term crop
  doesn't surface. No obvious on-page cause (canonical, robots, and schema all check out normally
  for this template) — worth a direct Search Console check for a crawl or manual-action issue, and
  a request-indexing in the meantime.
- **`/guides/tomato-blight` doesn't surface under its own URL — and `/blight-watch` is the page
  actually ranking for blight queries.** This looks like straightforward cannibalization between
  two pages targeting the same intent (one an explainer guide, one a live-data tool). Worth
  deciding which page should own the "tomato blight" query and linking the other in support of it,
  rather than leaving both to compete.
- **`/guides/composting` doesn't surface; `/blog/best-compost-bins-uk` does.** Similar overlap
  pattern to the blight case — worth checking whether the guide and the blog post are competing for
  the same query.
- **`/sow-in/london`, `/manchester`, `/birmingham`, `/leeds` — no evidence found; only Leicester
  and Worcester (much lower-volume cities) surfaced at all.** This is a rough but real data point
  for CUT_LIST §4's pending decision on the 51-city cluster: the pages are deliberately unlinked
  from nav/footer/homepage already (confirmed by `search-console-readiness.test.ts`), and this
  week's spot-check suggests the cluster genuinely isn't indexing well as a result — consistent
  with, not proof of, the "hold for Search Console export" plan already in place.
- **`/grow-your-christmas-dinner` and `/guides/growing-tomatoes-outdoors-vs-greenhouse` — no
  evidence found.** The Christmas page ties to finding #2 above; both are worth request-indexing
  once linked/reviewed.
- General `site:whattosow.co.uk` sampling this week was dominated by `/crops/*` and `/guides/*`
  results, with `/sow-in/*` and most `/sow/[month]` pages largely absent from the sample — consistent
  with the pattern above.

**Request-indexing priority this week (highest first):** `/sow/september` → `/crops/tomatoes` →
`/sow-in/london` → `/guides/composting` and `/guides/tomato-blight` (after resolving the
cannibalization question) → `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` (once linked) →
`/guides/growing-tomatoes-outdoors-vs-greenhouse`.

## OPPORTUNITIES (current UK season: mid/late September – early October)

1. **Leaf mould — genuine, clean content gap.** Well-established recurring October query, heavily
   covered by RHS-adjacent and competitor UK gardening publishers with near-identical seasonal
   timing (bin bags vs. wire cage, shredding, 1–2 year breakdown). No dedicated page or section
   exists anywhere on the site — checked `/guides/soil`, `/guides/green-manures` and
   `/guides/composting` directly, all silent on it. Natural home: a new section in
   `/guides/preparing-your-plot-for-winter` (already covers no-dig mulching, the same idea's
   cousin), or a short standalone guide alongside `/guides/green-manures`.
2. **Frost protection — the site's strongest existing match, just needs an indexing check.**
   `/frost-map` (confirmed well-indexed) and `/guides/protecting-vegetables-from-frost` already
   cover this well, with genuine regional differentiation (postcode/region-specific timing) that
   beats competitors' static date tables. The guide itself didn't surface directly in search this
   week (same pattern as composting/tomato-blight above) — worth confirming it's indexed before
   counting this as fully captured.
3. **Autumn garlic and onion planting — content-complete, indexing unconfirmed.** Heavily covered
   right now by UK retailers/publishers, and `/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions` already covers
   the Oct–Nov window and clove-division detail directly. `/crops/onion-sets` is confirmed indexed;
   the combined guide itself didn't surface on its own URL — worth a request-indexing rather than
   new content.
4. Carried forward, unchanged, from 1–8 September (not re-verified fresh this week, but nothing in
   the site has changed that would affect them): **green tomatoes at season's end** and **storing
   apples** remain real, evidenced content gaps against `/guides/tomato-blight` /
   `/guides/growing-tomatoes-outdoors-vs-greenhouse` and `/guides/growing-fruit` respectively.
