## FIX NOW (most impactful first)

### 1. The whole `/sow-in` cluster is orphaned from the rest of the site (52 pages)
- **Evidence:** `/sow-in` and the ~51 `/sow-in/<city>` pages only link to *each other*. Nothing in the header, footer, homepage, crop pages or guides links into the cluster (checked across `src/`, and the live homepage has no `/sow-in` link). The only other route in is `LocationSearch.tsx:43`, but that's a JavaScript `window.location` redirect used only on `/sow-in` itself, and Google doesn't follow it.
- **Why it matters:** Google only finds these pages through the sitemap, so they carry almost no internal link weight. That makes them look even thinner than CUT_LIST already worries they are.
- **Fix (pick one, since CUT_LIST ⛔ gates the bigger call):**
  - *Keep them:* add one crawlable link into the hub, e.g. a Footer "Explore" line `<Link href="/sow-in">Sowing by town</Link>` (`src/components/Footer.tsx` ~line 84). Then on each `/crops/[slug]` page, add a single "Sowing near you: Leeds · Bristol · …" line linking 3–4 cities. Don't build a link farm (ANTI_PATTERNS).
  - *Or cut them:* if Kate's CUT_LIST decision is to remove them, orphaned is the right state *only* alongside a redirect or noindex and removal from `sitemap.ts`. Don't leave them half-alive.

### 2. `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` is orphaned, not indexed, and heading into its season
- **Evidence:** no page in `src/` links to it (the only mention is `src/lib/seasonal-route-policy.ts`, which isn't a link). The live homepage doesn't link it. `site:` search finds nothing from us, while Suttons, Kitchen Garden, Two Wests and Growseed all rank for "grow your own Christmas dinner". The page itself is strong: ~1,200 words, one H1, FAQ.
- **Fix:**
  1. Link it in context from `/guides/autumn-winter-vegetables`, `/guides/sow-now-eat-all-winter`, `/crops/brussels-sprouts` and `/crops/parsnips` with the anchor **"grow your Christmas dinner"**.
  2. From October to December, add it to the homepage's seasonal links, next to the autumn "Sow now for autumn & winter" eyebrow (`src/app/page.tsx` ~line 100).
  3. The page currently links out only via nav and footer. Link each crop on the "plate" to its `/crops/<slug>` page so link weight flows both ways.
  4. Then **request indexing** **[SC]**.

### 3. `/still-time` is orphaned, not indexed, and thin
- **Evidence:** zero inbound links in `src/` and none on the live homepage. `site:` search finds nothing. The live page has ~150–200 words of body text, and most of the content appears to be client-rendered.
- **Fix:** either (a) link it from `/sow` and from the month pages (`/sow/[month]`) with the anchor **"what you can still sow this week"**, and add ~150 words of server-rendered intro plus the current closing-window crops as plain HTML, or (b) if it duplicates `/sow` (CUT_LIST item 9, ⛔ "one of each thing"), remove it from `sitemap.ts` and redirect it to `/sow`. Pick one this week.

### 4. The garlic & onion autumn guide is weakly linked, mistitled for the season, and not indexed
- **Page:** `/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions` (`src/app/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions/page.tsx:10-12`)
- **Evidence:**
  - It has only two inbound links: the `/guides` index and `/guides/growing-onions-garlic-leeks`.
  - It's **missing from `src/lib/guide-relevance.ts` META**, so `guideScore()` returns 0 and the "living" `/guides` index ranks it at the bottom **in its own peak month**.
  - `site:` search finds no result for it (or for `/crops/garlic`; only the variety page `/crops/garlic/lautrec-wight` shows).
  - The title and description are still July/August "order now" copy: *"UK suppliers open their autumn garlic and onion set catalogues in late July and August…"*. The description is ~250 characters (Google cuts off at ~155).
  - Every competitor on page one for "when to plant garlic UK" uses a "When to plant garlic" title (Garden UK, Glorious Garden, Bolton Turf, Growli).
- **Fix:**
  1. Add `"/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions": { months: [8, 9, 10], kit: true },` to `guide-relevance.ts` META (after line 86).
  2. Change the title to **"When to Plant Garlic & Onion Sets in Autumn (UK)"** (48 characters; the site-name suffix will be cut off anyway).
  3. Change the description to something like *"Plant garlic from mid-October to November and overwintering onion sets from September. When to plant where you are, hardneck vs softneck, and what to buy."* (~150 characters). Check it against tone-of-voice.md first.
  4. Link it from `/crops/garlic` and `/crops/onion-sets` with the anchor "planting garlic and onions in autumn".
  5. Request indexing for both the guide and `/crops/garlic` **[SC]**.

### 5. Monthly blog posts compete with `/sow/<month>` for the same query
- **Evidence:** `/blog/what-to-sow-in-<month>-2026` (title from `src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx:77`: "What to Sow in September 2026 — UK Sowing Guide") and `/sow/<month>` (`src/app/sow/[month]/page.tsx:37`: "What to Sow in September — UK Planting Guide") are built from the same crop data and go after the same query. Neither `/blog/what-to-sow-in-september-2026` nor `/sow/october` showed up in `site:` searches. The October post goes live on 1 October, so that's a third page on "what to sow in October".
- **Fix:** choose `/sow/<month>` as the evergreen page for "what to sow in <month>". Canonicalise each monthly blog post to its `/sow/<month>` page (or redirect the posts and drop them from `getPublishedPosts()` in the sitemap). Keep the blog for the hand-written diary and editorial posts. **[SC]**: the Performance → Queries report for "what to sow in september" will show which URL Google has actually picked.

### 6. City page titles go stale in search results
- **Evidence:** search results show `/sow-in/leicester` as *"What to Sow in Leicester — June 2026 Sowing Guide"*, three months out of date, because the month and year are baked into `<title>` (`src/app/sow-in/[city]/page.tsx:49`).
- **Fix:** if the pages stay (see #1), use a timeless title like **"What to Sow in Leicester — Sowing Calendar & Frost Dates"** and keep the month in the body only.

### 7. Two H1s on the homepage, plus a hidden H1 on every page
- `src/app/page.tsx:269` and `:299` both render an `<h1>` (mobile and desktop versions), and both are in the HTML. Make one of them a `<p>` or `<div>` with the same styling.
- `src/app/layout.tsx:95` puts `<h1>What To Sow</h1>` inside `<noscript>`, so every page technically has a second H1. Change it to a `<p>`.
- `src/app/harvest-planner/page.tsx:54` has a print-only second `<h1>`. Change it to a `<p>` (low priority).

### 8. Titles too long for search results (~25 pages)
Google shows ~60 characters. These titles run to 70–81 characters, so the useful end gets cut off. Shorten the seasonal ones first:
- Crop playbook template `src/app/crops/[slug]/page.tsx:429`: "How to Grow Tomatoes in the UK — When to Plant, Care & Problems | What To Sow" (78). → **"How to Grow {Crop} in the UK: When to Plant & Problems"**. Also fix the casing: search results show "How to Grow Runner **b**eans".
- `/guides/growing-squash-pumpkins-courgettes` (~80), `/guides/growing-brassicas` (~80), `/guides/growing-root-vegetables` (~81), `/guides/growing-onions-garlic-leeks` (~76), `/guides/tomato-blight` (~77, and "Hutton Criteria" is jargon nobody searches for), `/guides/succession-sowing` (~76), `/guides/composting` (~75), `/guides/autumn-winter-vegetables` (~72), `/guides/protecting-vegetables-from-frost` (~70), `/guides/overwintering-broad-beans-and-peas` (~70), `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` (~70).
- Quick rule: drop the "(UK Guide)" and "— The X Family" padding first, and let " | What To Sow" be the part that gets cut.

### 9. `keywords` meta arrays still everywhere (CUT_LIST says stop)
~102 `keywords:` entries across 57 files, including `src/app/layout.tsx:32`, every dynamic template (`crops/[slug]:434`, `sow-in/[city]:51`, `sow/[month]:39`, `blog/[slug]:58,82`), 28 in `src/data/editorial-posts.ts` and 18 in `src/data/companion-topics.ts`. They're harmless but dead weight. Remove them in one sweep; no ranking impact either way.

### 10. Smaller items
- Sitemap pages with **no JSON-LD**: `/sow`, `/grow`, `/harvest`, `/crops`, `/frost-map`, `/harvest-planner`, `/us`. At minimum add `BreadcrumbList`; `/crops` could carry an `ItemList` of crops. Low priority. Guides are well covered (most carry 3 schema blocks).
- `/kit` is `noindex` **and** canonicalises to `/guides` (`src/app/kit/page.tsx:9,30`). These are mixed signals. Keep the noindex and drop the canonical.
- `sitemap.ts` `lastModified` dates are hard-coded (mostly `2026-04-02`) although city, crop and variety pages now refresh hourly. Google ignores `lastmod` values that are always wrong. Either use real edit dates or leave `lastModified` out.
- The sitemap is generated at build time, so the October monthly blog post won't be listed until the next deploy (only relevant if #5 keeps the monthly posts).
