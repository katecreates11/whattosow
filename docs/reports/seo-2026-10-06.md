## FIX NOW (most impactful first)

### 1. Homepage ships two literal `<h1>` elements with identical text
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx:269` and `:299`. Both say "Know exactly / what to plant, / right now" — one in a `sm:hidden` mobile block, one in `hidden sm:flex` desktop block. This is CSS `display`, not conditional rendering, so **both land in the server-rendered HTML** Google reads, on the single highest-value URL on the site. **Fix:** change one to a `<p>`/`<div>` with the same classes — visually nothing changes, the duplicate heading does. Two bites at this already (flagged 29 Sept, still live).

### 2. `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` is orphaned and its season has moved closer
**File:** `src/app/grow-your-christmas-dinner/page.tsx`. Confirmed again this week: zero contextual links anywhere in `src/` (only hits are `seasonal-route-policy.ts`, a cache config, not a link). It only exists in the DOM it renders itself. It's in the sitemap at priority 0.8 with strong content (~1,200 words, FAQ schema, a working "Christmas plate" tool) — it just has no way in. Flagged 29 Sept when Christmas was 3 months out; today it's 2.5 months out and still has no door.
**Fix:** one contextual link each from `/guides/autumn-winter-vegetables`, `/crops/brussels-sprouts`, `/crops/parsnips`, anchor text "grow your Christmas dinner" — then a homepage seasonal link through December. Then **[SC]** request indexing.

### 3. `/kit`'s canonical still points at the wrong URL
**File:** `src/app/kit/page.tsx:27,30` — `openGraph.url` and `alternates.canonical` both say `/guides`, not `/kit`. The page is `noindex` but `follow: true`, so crawl/link equity still flows through it under the wrong URL. **Fix:** point both at `/kit`, or drop the canonical entirely since `noindex` already does the real work. One-line fix, flagged last week too.

### 4. `/privacy` still isn't in the sitemap
**File:** `src/app/sitemap.ts` — `/privacy` has its own real metadata and canonical, isn't `noindex`, but is the one page with genuine content that `sitemap.ts` never lists. (`/kit` and the parked personal-garden pages are *correctly* absent — there's a guard test for those — `/privacy` has no such exclusion, it's just missing.) **Fix:** add one entry to the static URL array.

### 5. `/admin/photos` is fully exposed with no metadata and no `noindex`
**File:** `src/app/admin/photos/page.tsx` — no `metadata` export at all, so it silently inherits the homepage's title, description and canonical (`/`), and nothing in `robots.ts` excludes `/admin`. It's unlinked internally so unlikely to be found by a crawl today, but there's no actual barrier if it ever is, or if someone shares the URL. **Fix:** give it `robots: { index: false, follow: false }` at minimum.

### 6. Guide titles and descriptions are systemically over-length — one root cause each
Not ~30 separate problems, two patterns:
- **Titles:** nearly every `/guides/*` static page (confirmed over 60 characters: `allotment-essentials`, `growing-root-vegetables`, `composting`, `growing-brassicas`, `growing-onions-garlic-leeks`, `protecting-vegetables-from-frost`, `succession-sowing`, `tomato-blight`, `pests`, `crop-rotation`, `sun-mapping`, `watering-while-away`, and ~15 more) plus **all 18** `/guides/companion-planting/[topic]` pages (data in `src/data/companion-topics.ts`, `metaTitle` field) run 65–81 characters. The pattern is always `<already-reasonable 50-55 char phrase> | What To Sow` — the 15-character site-name suffix is what tips almost every one of them over Google's ~60-character display limit. **Fix:** drop the `| What To Sow` suffix on guide pages (it gets truncated anyway) rather than rewriting 48 individual titles.
- **Descriptions:** the same ~30 guide pages plus all 18 companion-planting topics run 160–233 characters against a ~155-character display limit — `autumn-planting-garlic-onions` is worst at 233. These read as a single long sentence each; **fix** is to cut each to one punchy sentence under 155 characters, not a rewrite of the content.

### 7. `/sow-in/[city]` titles churn every month — a stability problem, not just a length one
**File:** `src/app/sow-in/[city]/page.tsx` — the title literally interpolates the *current* month/year at render time (`revalidate = 3600`), so the same URL's indexed title reads "...— June 2026 Sowing Guide" one month and "...— October 2026 Sowing Guide" the next. Google re-crawls, re-snippets and potentially re-ranks the same URL every time this changes — bad for the ~51 pages already flagged as borderline-thin (see below). **Fix:** a timeless title ("What to Sow in Leicester — Sowing Calendar & Frost Dates"), month stays in the body copy only.

### 8. `keywords` meta arrays remain in ~55 files, including `layout.tsx`
Full list confirmed by direct grep this week — essentially every guide page, every dynamic template (`crops/[slug]`, `sow-in/[city]`, `sow/[month]`, `blog/[slug]`, `companion-planting/[topic]`), and most tool pages. Per `docs/CUT_LIST.md`, Google has ignored this tag since 2009 — zero ranking impact either way, but it's dead weight worth one global strip rather than fixing file-by-file as you touch them.

### 9. `/crops` and `/sow` — the two most important hub pages — carry no Article/CollectionPage schema
Both are priority-0.9 sitemap entries. `/crops` has no JSON-LD at all; neither does `/sow`. The 181 variety pages and 12 month pages also carry breadcrumb schema only, no `Article`. Lower urgency than 1–7, but worth an `ItemList`/`CollectionPage` pass on the two hubs specifically — they're the pages most likely to earn rich results.

### 10. Smaller, confirmed-again items
- `/still-time` is still orphaned (zero internal links, reachable only via sitemap crawl) and still only ~60 lines of mostly-template content. CUT_LIST already gates the real fix (merge into `/sow`) behind Kate's approval — this is just confirming it's still in the same half-alive state.
- `InSeasonBand` component: confirmed still completely unimported anywhere in the codebase. CUT_LIST says "verify with grep, then delete" — verified; it's dead code ready to delete whenever that's approved.
- `/products`: confirmed fully dead — not in the sitemap, zero internal links anywhere, nothing left but the page file itself. Ready for the CUT_LIST §1 removal whenever Kate signs off.
- `/pins` and `/pins/c/[slug]`: zero links in from the main site (by design, for Pinterest referral traffic) — flagging only to confirm that's the intended shape, since right now nothing on whattosow.co.uk itself can reach them.

## OPPORTUNITIES (October, UK)

### 1. "When to plant garlic UK" — still a near-miss, now mid-peak
Second week flagging this: the planting window (mid-October–November) is exactly now, competitors (Garden UK, Glorious Garden, Bolton Turf, Growli) all title their pages "When to plant garlic in the UK", and `/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions` still titles itself **"Order Garlic & Onion Sets Now — Autumn Planting UK"** — ordering copy, not planting-query copy, and still missing from `guide-relevance.ts` so it doesn't surface on the live `/guides` index in its own best month. Same fix as last week (retitle to lead with "when to plant", add the `guide-relevance.ts` entry, link from `/crops/garlic`) — just more urgent with three fewer weeks of the window left.

### 2. End-of-season green tomatoes — a genuine content gap, not a near-miss
Evidence: "what to do with green tomatoes" / "ripen tomatoes indoors before frost" is a well-established, high-intent October query (epicgardening, growagoodlife, Cornell Extension all rank, but none UK-specific) — and the codebase has **zero** mentions of "green tomato" or "ripen indoors" anywhere (`tomato-blight` and `growing-veg-in-containers` don't cover it). This is real seasonal anxiety right now (first frosts, vines still loaded) with no dominant UK voice owning it. Per the "depth and links only" rule in `docs/NEXT_PHASES.md`, this doesn't need a new URL — a section added to `/guides/tomato-blight` or the tomato crop page, cross-linked from both, would do it.

### 3. Bare-root fruit & hedging season (Nov–March) starts imminently
Evidence: UK nurseries and the trade press mark November as the start of bare-root season; `/guides/growing-fruit` mentions "bare root" but has no dedicated seasonal hook or clear "the window opens now" framing, and there's commercial intent here (trees, hedging) that fits the existing affiliate model. **Improve**, don't build new: add a clear seasonal section to `growing-fruit`, cross-link from `/sow/november` and `/sow/october`.
