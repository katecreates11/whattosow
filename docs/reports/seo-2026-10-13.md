## FIX NOW (most impactful first — unchanged items re-verified in the files, not just asserted)

### 1. Homepage ships two literal `<h1>` elements with identical text
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx:269` (mobile, `sm:hidden`) and `:299` (desktop, `hidden sm:flex`). Both render "Know exactly / what to plant, / right now" into the server HTML — this is a CSS show/hide, not conditional rendering, so Google sees two H1s on the site's highest-value URL. **Fix:** change one to a `<p>`/`<div>` with the same classes; no visual change. Third week flagging this.

### 2. `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` is still orphaned, three weeks from its moment
**File:** `src/app/grow-your-christmas-dinner/page.tsx`. Re-confirmed by grep: the only hits for this slug in `src/` are the page itself, the sitemap, and `seasonal-route-policy.ts` (a cache config, not a link) — nothing links to it anywhere on the site. ~1,200 words, FAQ schema, sitemap priority 0.8, and a working "Christmas plate" tool, with no door in. **Fix:** one contextual link each from `/guides/autumn-winter-vegetables`, `/crops/brussels-sprouts`, `/crops/parsnips`; a homepage seasonal link through December. Then **[SC]** request indexing.

### 3. `/kit`'s canonical still points at `/guides`
**File:** `src/app/kit/page.tsx:27` (`openGraph.url`) and `:30` (`alternates.canonical`) both say `/guides`. The page is `noindex, follow: true`, so crawl equity still flows out under the wrong URL. **Fix:** point both at `/kit`, or drop the canonical since `noindex` already does the real work. One line. Fourth week flagging this.

### 4. `/privacy` still isn't in the sitemap
**File:** `src/app/sitemap.ts`. `/privacy` has real metadata, a real canonical, isn't `noindex`, and is linked from the footer — but `sitemap.ts`'s static URL array never lists it. `/kit` and the personal-garden pages are *correctly* excluded (there's a guard test); `/privacy` has no such exclusion, it's just missing. **Fix:** add one entry.

### 5. `/admin/photos` is fully exposed with no metadata and no `noindex`
**File:** `src/app/admin/photos/page.tsx` — no `metadata` export, so it silently inherits the homepage's title/description/canonical. `src/app/robots.ts` blocks a named list of AI-training bots but has no `/admin` rule for anyone else. Unlinked internally, so unlikely to be crawled today, but there's no actual barrier if the URL ever leaks or gets linked by mistake. **Fix:** `metadata = { robots: { index: false, follow: false } }` at minimum.

### 6. Guide titles and descriptions are systemically over-length — two root causes, not ~48 edits
- **Titles:** nearly every `/guides/*` static page and all 18 `/guides/companion-planting/[topic]` pages (`metaTitle` in `src/data/companion-topics.ts`) run 65–81 characters. The pattern is always `<reasonable 50–55 char phrase> | What To Sow` — the 15-character suffix tips almost every one past Google's ~60-character display limit. **Fix:** drop the `| What To Sow` suffix on guide pages.
- **Descriptions:** the same pages run 160–233 characters against a ~155-character display limit (`autumn-planting-garlic-onions` worst at 233). **Fix:** one punchy sentence under 155 characters each, not a content rewrite.

### 7. `/sow-in/[city]` titles bake in the current month/year at render time
**File:** `src/app/sow-in/[city]/page.tsx` (`revalidate = 3600`) — the indexed title for the same URL reads differently every month ("...— October 2026 Sowing Guide" today, something else in November). That's unnecessary re-crawl/re-snippet churn on 51 pages already on the CUT_LIST thin-content watch. **Fix:** a timeless title ("What to Sow in Leicester — Sowing Calendar & Frost Dates"); keep month/year in body copy only.

### 8. `keywords` meta arrays remain in 57 files, including `layout.tsx`
Recounted by direct grep this week: 102 occurrences across 57 files (`layout.tsx`, every guide, every dynamic template — `crops/[slug]`, `sow-in/[city]`, `sow/[month]`, `blog/[slug]`, `companion-planting/[topic]`). Zero ranking impact per `docs/CUT_LIST.md` (Google's ignored this tag since 2009) — dead weight, worth one global strip.

### 9. `/crops` and `/sow` carry no Article/CollectionPage schema
Both are priority-0.9 sitemap entries — the two most important hub pages — with no JSON-LD at all. The 181 variety pages and 12 month pages carry breadcrumb schema only, no `Article`. Lower urgency than 1–7, but these are the pages most likely to earn rich results.

### 10. Smaller, reconfirmed items
- `/still-time` — still orphaned (grep: only self-references, sitemap, and a test file link to it), still thin. CUT_LIST gates the real fix (merge into `/sow`) on Kate's approval.
- `InSeasonBand` — confirmed still unimported anywhere. Dead code, ready to delete on approval.

- `/pins` and `/pins/c/[slug]` — still zero links in from the main site, which looks intentional (Pinterest referral traffic) rather than a bug. Flagging only to confirm that's the intended shape.

## OPPORTUNITIES (October, UK)

### 1. "When to plant garlic UK" — third week flagging, window now genuinely closing
Evidence unchanged and worsening: mid-October–November is the planting window, competitors (Garden UK, Glorious Garden, Bolton Turf, Growli) all title their pages "When to plant garlic in the UK", and `/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions` still titles itself "Order Garlic & Onion Sets Now — Autumn Planting UK" — ordering copy, not planting-query copy — and still isn't in `guide-relevance.ts`, so it doesn't surface on the site's own `/guides` index this month. Same fix as the last two weeks, now with less runway left to act on it.

### 2. End-of-season green tomatoes — still a genuine gap, not a near-miss
"What to do with green tomatoes" / "ripen tomatoes indoors before frost" remains a well-established, high-intent October UK query (epicgardening, growagoodlife, Cornell Extension rank; no dominant UK-specific voice), and the codebase still has zero mentions of "green tomato" or "ripen indoors" anywhere. Real seasonal anxiety right now with first frosts approaching and vines still loaded. Fits as a section added to `/guides/tomato-blight` or the tomato crop page — no new URL needed.

### 3. New this week: Halloween pumpkin seeds/carving has zero coverage on an existing, already-decent page
Evidence: UK press coverage of pumpkin waste at Halloween is substantial (one widely-cited estimate: ~18,000 tonnes of edible pumpkin wasted annually in the UK), and "what to do with pumpkin seeds" / "roast pumpkin seeds after carving" is a recurring high-intent late-October query with UK lifestyle sites (various regional papers, Co-op's own blog) actively ranking for it. `/guides/growing-squash-pumpkins-courgettes` already covers growing pumpkins in depth but has zero mention of carving, seeds, or Halloween — a one-paragraph addition ("don't bin the seeds — toast them...") with a line linking out to the harvest/eating side would catch a real seasonal search spike on a page that already exists and is indexed, two and a half weeks before the peak.
