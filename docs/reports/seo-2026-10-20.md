## FIX NOW (most impactful first)

### 1. Homepage still ships two literal `<h1>` elements with identical text
**File:** `src/app/page.tsx:269` (mobile, `sm:hidden`) and `:299` (desktop, `hidden sm:flex`). Both render "Know exactly / what to plant, / right now" — a CSS show/hide, not conditional rendering, so Google sees two H1s on the site's highest-value URL. **Fix:** change one to a `<p>`/`<div>` with the same classes; no visual change. Fourth week flagging this.

### 2. `/kit`'s canonical and OG url still point at `/guides`
**File:** `src/app/kit/page.tsx:27` (`openGraph.url`) and `:30` (`alternates.canonical`) both say `https://whattosow.co.uk/guides` / `/guides`. The page is `robots: { index: false, follow: true }` (line 8), so crawl equity still flows out under the wrong URL via `follow: true`. **Fix:** point both at `/kit`, or drop the canonical since `noindex` already does the real work. Fifth week flagging this.

### 3. `/privacy` still isn't in the sitemap
**File:** `src/app/sitemap.ts`. `/privacy` has real metadata (`title: "Privacy Policy — What To Sow"`, its own `canonical: "/privacy"`), isn't `noindex`, and is linked from the footer (`src/components/Footer.tsx:87`) — but `sitemap.ts`'s static URL array never lists it. `/kit`, `/my-garden`, `/my-plot`, `/lucky-dip`, `/products` are *correctly* excluded (there's a guard test); `/privacy` has no such exclusion, it's just missing. **Fix:** add one entry.

### 4. `/admin/photos` is fully exposed with no metadata and no `noindex`
**File:** `src/app/admin/photos/page.tsx` — confirmed again, no `metadata` export at all, so it silently inherits the homepage's title/description/canonical. `src/app/robots.ts` blocks a named list of AI-training bots by name but has no `/admin` disallow rule for anyone else. Unlinked internally, so unlikely to be crawled today, but there's no actual barrier if the URL ever leaks or gets linked by mistake. **Fix:** `metadata = { robots: { index: false, follow: false } }` at minimum.

### 5. Footer still has no link into `/sow-in`
**File:** `src/components/Footer.tsx` — re-read in full this week: the "Grow" and "Explore" columns (lines 69–76, 84–88) link `/crops`, `/calendar`, `/sow`, `/guides`, `/`, `/us`, `/guides/companion-planting-chart`, `/blog`, `/frost-map`, `/#common-questions`, `/about`, `/privacy`, `/feed.xml` — thirteen links, none to `/sow-in`. The 51 city pages (priority 0.7 in the sitemap) have no entry point from site navigation at all; they're reachable only by direct URL or internal link from elsewhere. **Fix:** one footer link to `/sow-in`.

### 6. `/grow-your-christmas-dinner` is still orphaned, now 11 days from Halloween and well inside its own Christmas-dinner planting window closing
**File:** `src/app/grow-your-christmas-dinner/page.tsx`. Re-confirmed by grep: the only hits for this slug anywhere in `src/` are the page itself, the sitemap, and `src/lib/seasonal-route-policy.ts` (a cache-revalidation config, not a link) — nothing links to it. ~1,200 words, FAQ schema, sitemap priority 0.8, a working "Christmas plate" tool. **Fix:** one contextual link each from `/guides/autumn-winter-vegetables`, `/crops/brussels-sprouts`, `/crops/parsnips`; a homepage seasonal link through December. Then **[SC]** request indexing. Fourth week flagging this.

### 7. Guide titles and descriptions remain systemically over-length — recounted precisely this week
- **Titles:** **26 of 34** static `/guides/*` pages run their title past ~60 characters (recount by script against the actual multi-line `title:` strings, not a single-line grep — last week's "~30 of ~37" was in the right range but imprecise). Pattern is always `<a reasonable 45–65 char phrase> | What To Sow` — the `| What To Sow` suffix (15 chars) is what tips most of them over. Worst: `/guides/growing-root-vegetables` at 79 chars.
- **Companion-planting topics are worse, not better, than last week's count suggested:** all **17 of 17** `/guides/companion-planting/[topic]` pages exceed 60 chars once the suffix is counted (`src/app/guides/companion-planting/[topic]/page.tsx:76` appends `` `${t.metaTitle} | What To Sow` `` at render time — the raw `metaTitle` values in `src/data/companion-topics.ts` look fine at 50–62 chars, but the rendered title is always 15 chars longer). Same root cause as the static guides.
- **Descriptions:** all **17 of 17** companion-topic `description` fields in `src/data/companion-topics.ts` exceed 155 characters (worst: lettuce, 215 chars).
- **Fix (one change, two places):** drop the `| What To Sow` suffix on guide pages and on the companion-topic template; trim the 17 companion descriptions to one punchy sentence each.

### 8. `/sow-in/[city]` titles still bake in the current month/year at render time
**File:** `src/app/sow-in/[city]/page.tsx` (`revalidate = 3600`) — the indexed title for the same URL reads differently every month ("...— October 2026 Sowing Guide" today). Unnecessary re-crawl/re-snippet churn on 51 pages already on the CUT_LIST thin-content watch. **Fix:** a timeless title ("What to Sow in Leicester — Sowing Calendar & Frost Dates"); keep month/year in body copy only.

### 9. `keywords` meta arrays remain in 57 files

Zero ranking impact per `docs/CUT_LIST.md` — Google's ignored this tag since 2009. Dead weight; worth one global strip whenever someone's next in these files anyway.

## OPPORTUNITIES (late October, UK)

### 1. "When to plant garlic UK" — fifth week flagging, window now more than half gone
Evidence unchanged: UK sources confirm October–December as the main autumn-planting window, with October–November called out as the better half of it (roots establish before hard frost). Competitors title their pages directly on the query ("When to plant garlic in the UK"). `/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions` still titles itself "Order Garlic & Onion Sets Now — Autumn Planting UK" — ordering copy, not planting-query copy — and still isn't surfaced in `guide-relevance.ts`, so it doesn't appear on the site's own `/guides` index this month either. Same fix as the last four weeks; less runway each time it's left.

### 2. Halloween pumpkin seeds/carving — still zero coverage, now 11 days out
Re-confirmed: `/guides/growing-squash-pumpkins-courgettes` has one line on winter squash curing but nothing on carving, seeds, or Halloween. "What to do with pumpkin seeds" / "roast pumpkin seeds after carving" remains a real, recurring late-October UK search spike (UK lifestyle and regional press cover it every year around the ~18,000-tonne pumpkin-waste figure). A one-paragraph addition ("don't bin the seeds — toast them...") to an existing, already-indexed page would catch it. Runway is short — worth doing this week if at all, not next.

### 3. New this week: "how to make leaf mould" — a genuine near-miss, not zero coverage
`/guides/composting` mentions "dry leaves" only generically as a brown/carbon input inside the hot-composting method (`src/app/guides/composting/page.tsx:160,335,494`) — it never describes leaf mould as its own, different process (slow fungal breakdown in a punctured bin bag, not the hot compost bin). RHS, Shropshire Wildlife Trust and several other UK sources actively rank for "how to make leaf mould" / "what to do with autumn leaves" right now — it's an evergreen November query that peaks with leaf-fall. A short, separate section in the composting guide (bag method, "next autumn it's ready," what leaves work best) would fill a real gap on a page that already exists and already ranks for composting generally.

### Retracted from last week
"Zero mentions of green tomato anywhere" (last week's Opportunity #2) — see correction at the top. The site already has good, on-voice coverage on `/crops/tomatoes` and `/guides/tomato-blight`; not re-flagged.
