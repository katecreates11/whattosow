## FIX NOW

Most impactful first. Each one names the exact file/page and the exact change.

1. **A live, indexed guide is now telling searchers to do something the calendar has already closed the window on.** `src/app/guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions/page.tsx` — title "**Order Garlic & Onion Sets Now** — Autumn Planting UK", description and body all say the ordering window is "late July and August," catalogues "dispatch from September," and urge "order in the next few weeks." Today is 17 November. Anyone landing here from search right now (the live intent is "is it too late to plant garlic," not "order now") gets a page that reads as out of date — a trust wound, not just a stale date. **Fix:** this is a seasonal evergreen page, not a one-off post — it needs a rewrite pass (or season-aware copy) before each autumn, or the title/intro need to stop promising "now" when the ordering window has passed. For this week specifically: the FAQ already has "Is it too late to order if I've missed late July?" — promote that answer to the top of the page and soften the title until the copy is refreshed. Flagging for Kate rather than editing, since this is content voice, not a technical fix.

2. **Title tags are badly oversized on the 4 flagship playbook crop pages — truncated in Google's results, brand name dropped.** `src/app/crops/[slug]/page.tsx` line ~429: `"How to Grow ${name} in the UK — When to Plant, Care & Problems | What To Sow"` runs 76–86 characters (Maincrop Potatoes = 86) against Google's ~60-char/580px practical cutoff. Affects tomatoes, carrots, courgettes, maincrop potatoes — the site's deepest, most-linked pages. **Fix:** shorten to `"How to Grow ${name} in the UK | What To Sow"` (~45–55 chars); move "When to Plant, Care & Problems" into the meta description.

3. **Same 4 crop pages: meta description is ~176 characters**, over the ~155–160 char SERP budget (line ~432). **Fix:** cut after "...what goes wrong and how to fix it." (~140 chars); drop the trailing "and the varieties worth growing" clause, which is getting cut off anyway.

4. **Meta descriptions are systemically over-length across 30 of the 34 `/guides/*` pages** — they truncate mid-sentence, losing the benefit clause search snippets rely on. Worst: `guides/autumn-planting-garlic-onions` (233 chars — also item 1 above), `guides/watering-while-away` (214), `guides/growing-root-vegetables` (213), `guides/growing-veg-in-containers` (211), `guides/growing-winter-salad-leaves` (210), `guides/protecting-vegetables-from-frost` (209), `guides/succession-sowing` (207), plus 23 more in the 162–204 range (companion-planting-chart, green-manures, growing-brassicas, growing-onions-garlic-leeks, growing-squash-pumpkins-courgettes, growing-tomatoes-outdoors-vs-greenhouse, overwintering-broad-beans-and-peas, preparing-your-plot-for-winter, seed-starting-kit, sow-now-eat-all-winter, sun-mapping, tomato-blight, what-to-sow-in-summer-uk, dealing-with-the-glut, autumn-winter-vegetables, allotment-essentials, composting, crop-rotation, growing-fruit, seed-starting, pests, microgreens-and-windowsill-herbs, blight-watch, frost-map, grow-your-christmas-dinner, longest-day, bed-planner). **Fix:** trim every `description` to ≤155 chars, keeping the query-matching lead clause and cutting the trailing padding.

5. **Titles are also over the ~60-char limit on ~28 of those same guide pages** — e.g. `growing-root-vegetables` (79 chars), `growing-squash-pumpkins-courgettes` (78), `growing-brassicas` (78), `tomato-blight` (76), `growing-onions-garlic-leeks` (75), `calendar` (74), `composting` (73), `succession-sowing` (73), `allotment-essentials` / `autumn-winter-vegetables` (71). `src/app/about/page.tsx`'s description is also 177 chars, same issue, just outside `/guides/`. **Fix:** shorten the descriptive clause, keep the " | What To Sow" suffix so the brand doesn't get dropped from the SERP.

6. **Heading stutter on every crop page: "Can I sow [crop] now?" appears twice.** `CropVerdictBand` renders a large styled line reading "Can I sow {crop} now?"; `CropNowAnswerBlock` renders a real `<h2>` with the identical phrase further down the same page. Same question, asked twice, per ANTI_PATTERNS' heading-stutter rule. **Fix:** vary one — keep the verdict band's phrasing, change the answer block's heading to something like "What to do about {crop} right now."

7. **`src/app/harvest/page.tsx`, `src/app/grow/page.tsx`, `src/app/sow/page.tsx` carry zero JSON-LD** — not even `BreadcrumbList` — despite being three of the highest-priority URLs in the sitemap (priority 0.9, weekly change). Every comparable top-level page (about, blog, calendar, guides index) has at least a breadcrumb. **Fix:** add `BreadcrumbList` to all three as a baseline; `harvest` and `sow` are also good `CollectionPage`/`ItemList` candidates since they render a list of crops.

8. **Inconsistent `Article` schema among the older guides.** `beginners`, `crop-rotation`, `growing-fruit`, `seed-starting`, `soil`, `watering`, `pests` carry `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` but no `Article`, while 20+ newer guides carry `Article` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` (and `tomato-blight` adds `HowTo`). **Fix:** add `Article` schema (headline/datePublished/author/publisher) to those 7 for consistency and article rich-result eligibility.

9. **Variety pages (181 of them) carry no structured data beyond breadcrumbs**, despite each having genuinely unique editorial content (a variety "personality" paragraph plus at least one original recipe — confirmed via sample and a full-file check: zero varieties have empty recipes). A cheap addition since the content already exists. **Fix:** add `Article`/`CreativeWork` schema to `src/app/crops/[slug]/[variety]/page.tsx`. Don't add `Product` schema — no verified price/availability data to back it.

10. **`keywords` meta arrays are still being set sitewide** — found on `src/app/layout.tsx` (the root default, inherited everywhere) plus explicit overrides on roughly 40 pages (about, bed-planner, blight-watch, blog, calendar, frost-map, grow-your-christmas-dinner, guides, harvest-planner, kit, longest-day, lucky-dip, my-garden, products, sow-in, still-time, ~25 of the 34 guides, plus the crop and city templates). Google has ignored this tag since 2009 and CUT_LIST already flags it. **Fix:** one cleanup commit, strip `keywords` from every metadata object sitewide — harmless to leave, but it's dead weight repeated on every single page.

11. **Minor/cosmetic:** `src/app/harvest-planner/page.tsx` and `src/app/print/page.tsx` each render a second `<h1>` inside a `hidden print:block` print-stylesheet header — invisible on screen (`display:none`), so low impact, but it's a genuine duplicate `<h1>` in the DOM. **Fix:** change the print-only heading to a styled `<p>`/`<span>` — no visual change.

### Orphan page / thin internal linking (high value — internal links drive indexing)

- **`/still-time` is a true orphan.** It's in `sitemap.ts` (priority 0.8, daily change-frequency) and **not** noindexed — fully eligible for indexing — but no `<Link>`/`href` anywhere in the codebase points to it except its own file. It's only reachable by typing the URL or a crawler finding it in the sitemap with nothing to crawl *to* it from. This is also the page CUT_LIST already slates for a redirect into `/sow` — right now it's live, indexable, and linkless, which is the worst of both states. **Fix:** either add one link to it today (from the homepage or `/sow`), or ship the planned redirect — don't leave it in this in-between state.
- **`/sow-in` (the city index) is a near-orphan.** The only link to it is the "← All locations" back-link rendered *inside* its own `/sow-in/[city]` children — nothing in nav, footer, homepage, or any guide links to the index page itself. **Fix:** one link from `/frost-map` or the footer's "Explore" column, pointing at the index, not just at individual cities.
- Everything else checked is healthy: all 51 city pages are linked from `/frost-map`'s table and from "nearby city" cross-links; all 181 variety pages are linked from their parent crop page; all 34 guides are listed on `/guides` and most cross-link a "next guide"; companion-planting topic pages link from their index, `/pins`, and sibling pages; blog posts link from `/blog`'s index (no related-article grids, which is correct per ANTI_PATTERNS, not a gap). `/kit`, `/bench`, `/pins`, `/my-plot`, `/my-garden`, `/products`, `/bed-planner`, `/print`, `/lucky-dip` are all correctly noindexed and absent from the sitemap — consistent by design, not an issue.

## OPPORTUNITIES

Evidence-based only — both checked against the repo first so they're genuine gaps, not duplicate work.

1. **"How to force rhubarb" — real UK search demand right now (RHS, Gardeners' World, Ideal Home, Homes & Gardens all have active guides), and the site's coverage is one sentence.** The rhubarb crop entry (`src/data/crops.ts`, Rhubarb) says only *"Force a clump in January for the most tender, pink stems"* — no method. The actual query people are typing this month wants: cover with a forcing pot/bucket/bin for total darkness, pack straw around the crown, expect stems in 4–8 weeks, rest a forced crown 2–3 years before forcing it again, 'Timperley Early' and 'Champagne' (both already in the site's variety list) are the ones that force best. **Fix:** deepen the existing rhubarb crop page with a short forcing section — this fits the site's own rule of depth over proliferation, no new page needed.
2. **"How to make leaf mould" — a classic November query (RHS, Saga, GrowVeg all rank for it) — the site only name-drops "leaf mould" as a soil ingredient, never explains how to make it.** Three mentions in `src/app/guides/soil/page.tsx` and `src/app/guides/pests/page.tsx` all treat it as something you already have, not something you make. The real how-to is short: wire-mesh bin or bin bags, collect now through autumn, mower-chopped leaves rot faster, avoid diseased fruit-tree/rose leaves and spiny holly/pyracantha, ready in 1–2 years. **Fix:** expand the existing "Leaf mould" subsection in `guides/soil` (line ~350) from a one-line mention into an actual short how-to — again, depth on an existing page rather than a new one.

Checked and **not** flagged as gaps (already well covered, so no recommendation): bare-root fruit tree planting (thorough in `guides/growing-fruit`), autumn garlic/onion planting (own guide — though see FIX NOW #1 for its staleness), overwintering broad beans (own guide), windowsill herbs/microgreens (brand-new guide, shipped 2026-11-16), chitting potatoes (that query's real season is Jan–Feb, not now), sweet peas and other ornamentals (outside this site's edible-crops focus, not a gap worth closing).
